01 About

Images with something to say

I'm a visual artist and creative professional working across digital content, brand communication, production design, photography and visual storytelling. My practise is rooted in composition, image-making and narrative, and in building visual identities that get an idea across clearly.

I've designed social posts, banners, covers and promotional content for brands and media, and created physical visual environments too. My Film Studies background shapes how I think about framing, colour and direction.
